01 · The problem

Every trade has two prices. Traders only see one.

The order window shows one price — the market's. Measured against it, quietly and every time, is another: fees, exchange charges, GST, funding, taxes. They never appear in the candles, so traders judge every trade on gross P&L — and wonder why the account balance keeps telling a smaller story.

This is attrition, not punishment. A strategy that "makes money" can still bleed money, because the first failure in trading isn't a bad call. It is judging the call before the bill arrives.

02 · The mathematics

Discipline is arithmetic, not attitude.

Tradingmaster treats cost-awareness as a computation, not a pep talk. Every tool runs the same three operations before entry:

01

Net-first position math

Risk budget is spent only after fees are modeled. Position notional is solved so that loss-at-stop — execution costs, funding estimates and withholding included — stays inside the risk you chose.

02

Locked, verified schedules

Trade variables are yours to enter; the arithmetic is ours to maintain. Fee schedules are read-only and re-verified against official sources — the same math, every time, for every trader.

03

Exact breakevens

Not approximations. Breakeven moves are solved to the paise, with every charge recomputed at the solution — so you know precisely where a trade stops losing money.

The equationEVERY TRADE · SOLVED BEFORE ENTRY
net edge=gross P&L−all costs>0

If the left side isn't positive, there is no trade — only a donation to the venue. Every Tradingmaster tool is this inequality, rendered to the paise.

03 · The goal

A generation of Indian traders who never ask "was it worth it?" twice.

One day, cost-awareness will be a reflex, not a feature. Traders will size from net, judge from net, and treat fee schedules as part of the strategy — across every exchange, every product, every account. Tradingmaster exists to bring that day closer: one tool, one calculation, one disciplined decision at a time.

Why it exists

Tradingmaster was built around a simple gap: many trading decisions begin with a target and end with a gross profit figure, while costs are considered too late. These calculators bring brokerage, exchange fees, GST, funding estimates, stop distance and other selected charges into the decision before entry.

The aim is not to predict markets or tell anyone what to trade. It is to make the arithmetic behind a trade easier to inspect.

What the tools cover

Crypto futures

The crypto futures calculator estimates a USDT-settled position notional from a chosen risk budget and stop distance, then applies locked entry and exit fee rates, GST, estimated funding and an optional TDS cash-withholding estimate.

Indian equity options

The options calculator separates gross P&L from brokerage, STT, exchange charges, SEBI turnover fees, stamp duty and GST. It also shows both a simple fee-recovery level and a dynamically solved breakeven.

How to use the output

  • Treat every result as an estimate, not an execution quote.
  • Review the visible assumptions; locked schedules are maintained by the Tradingmaster team and re-verified periodically — confirm that the displayed rates apply to your exchange, product, jurisdiction and tax position.
  • Use the result alongside independent research, official schedules and your own risk limits.
Editorial principleSpecific numbers should be visible, sourced and open to challenge. If a rate cannot be verified for your trade, do not treat the preset as authoritative.

Independence

Tradingmaster is an educational calculator, not a broker, exchange, investment adviser, portfolio manager, research analyst or tax adviser. No calculator result is a recommendation, signal, promise of performance or instruction to enter a trade.

No investment advice

Derivatives and leverage risk

Futures and options are complex, high-risk instruments. Leverage can magnify losses rapidly. You may lose all capital committed to a position and, depending on the product, market gap, liquidation process and account terms, may owe more than the margin posted. Stops may not execute at the selected price.

Never treat “risk budget” as a guaranteed maximum loss.Slippage, gaps, low liquidity, exchange controls, liquidation mechanics, fees and technical failure can make realized loss larger than the estimate.

Model limitations

Outputs depend entirely on the values supplied. Preset charges reflect the stated September 2026 model, not a promise that those rates apply now or to your account. The calculators may omit charges, rebates, tiered pricing, spread, slippage, conversion costs, exercise or assignment effects, penalties, settlement differences and account-specific terms.

Funding can be paid or received and can change by contract and market conditions. Breakeven results are mathematical estimates and do not account for every execution condition.

Tax and regulatory uncertainty

Tax treatment depends on the instrument, transaction structure, jurisdiction and individual circumstances. The applicability of 1% TDS to USDT-settled crypto futures is disputed; the optional TDS field is a cash-withholding scenario, not a conclusion about final tax liability. Consult a qualified professional and current official guidance.

Options-specific risks

The options calculator models a premium purchase followed by a premium sale before expiry. It does not model every outcome involving exercise, assignment, physical settlement, margin shortfall, close-out, auto square-off or expiry. In-the-money expiry can lead to materially different STT and settlement consequences.

Third parties

Broker, exchange and product names are used for identification. Tradingmaster is not endorsed by, affiliated with or acting for those third parties unless explicitly stated. Verify fees, contract terms, outages and regulatory notices directly with the relevant official source.

Your decision

You remain solely responsible for deciding whether to trade and for verifying the suitability, legality and economics of any transaction. Consider obtaining independent professional advice. If you do not understand the product or cannot afford the potential loss, do not trade it.
